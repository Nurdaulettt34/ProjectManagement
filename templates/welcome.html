This short tour takes less than a minute.

⬆

1. Upload a document

Drag a PDF or DOCX file (up to 20 MB) into My documents. The original is stored securely and is visible only to you.

Tip: scans without a text layer can't be read yet — use the original digital file when possible.

🧠

2. We recognize it

The assistant detects the document type and shows how confident it is. If it isn't sure, the document is marked Other instead of guessing.

🧾

3. Check the key details

Names, dates and numbers are pulled out for you.

  • Unclear found, but please double-check
  • Missing not in the document — left empty, never invented
  • Corrected you fixed it yourself

When everything looks right, press Mark as reviewed.

📝

4. Get a short summary

Press Summarize to get 3–7 points: deadlines, requirements and the documents you need. Everything comes from the document's own text.

Your profile shows your account details and document statistics. Requests to university offices are coming in the next release.
